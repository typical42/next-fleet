# User manual

NextFleet keeps a logbook for your vehicles in Nextcloud.
This manual uses the labels of the English interface.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> NextFleet helps you keep a logbook.
> It does not promise that a tax office, an auditor or a court accepts it.
> Nobody did a legal review of the app.

## Get started

This takes about five minutes.

### Add a vehicle

1. Click **NextFleet** in the top bar.
2. Click **New vehicle**.
3. Type the **Registration plate**, the **Manufacturer** and the **Model**.
4. In **Engine**, select the engine, for example **Petrol**.
5. In **Counter reading**, type the reading on the dashboard.
6. Click **Add vehicle**.

   The vehicle page opens.
   The **Odometer** tile shows your reading.

All fields are optional.
You can add the rest later with **Edit vehicle**.
Without an engine, you cannot record fuel or charging yet.

### Record a fill-up

1. On the vehicle page, click **New entry**.
2. In **Entry type**, click **Energy**.
3. Type the **Amount (l)** and the **Total price**.
4. In **Counter reading**, type the reading on the dashboard.
5. Click **Save**.

   The entry shows at the top of the **Timeline**.

After the second full tank, NextFleet shows the consumption.

## Words

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Entry | One record on a vehicle: a trip, an energy entry, a maintenance record, an expense or a counter reading. |
| Energy entry | A fill-up of fuel or a charge of the battery. |
| Counter | The odometer in kilometres, or the hour meter in engine hours. |
| Timeline | All entries of a vehicle, newest first. |
| Owner | The user who added the vehicle. |
| Access | The right of another user or group to see or use a vehicle. |
