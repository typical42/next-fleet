# NextFleet user manual

NextFleet is a logbook for your vehicles in Nextcloud. It records the counter, trips, fuel and
charging, maintenance and costs. It reminds you of inspections and services. You can give other
users access to a vehicle.

This manual is for the users of the app. It uses the labels of the English user interface.

## Get started

This tutorial adds a vehicle, records an energy entry and shows the result. It takes approximately
five minutes.

### Add a vehicle

1. Click **NextFleet** in the top navigation bar.

   The page shows **No vehicles yet**.
2. Click **New vehicle**.
3. In the **Registration plate** field, type the plate of your vehicle.
4. Type the **Manufacturer** and the **Model**.
5. In the **Engine** list, select the engine, for example **Petrol**.
6. In the **Counter reading** field, type the current counter reading.
7. Click **Add vehicle**.

   NextFleet opens the vehicle page. The **Odometer** tile shows your counter reading.

All fields are optional. You can add the data later with **Edit vehicle**.

### Record an energy entry

1. On the vehicle page, click **New entry**.
2. In **Entry type**, click **Energy**.
3. Type the **Amount (l)** and the **Total price**.
4. In the **Counter reading** field, type the counter reading from the dashboard.
5. Click **Save**.

   The energy entry shows at the top of the **Timeline**.

After the second full tank, the vehicle page shows the consumption.

## Words in this manual

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Entry | One record on a vehicle: a trip, an energy entry, a maintenance record, an expense or a counter reading. |
| Energy entry | A fill-up of fuel or a charge of the battery. |
| Counter | The odometer in kilometres, or the hour meter in engine hours. |
| Counter reading | The value of the counter at one time. |
| Timeline | The list of all entries of a vehicle, newest first. |
| Owner | The user who added the vehicle. |
| Access | The right of another user or group to see or use a vehicle. |

The developers use the same words in [CONTEXT.md](../../CONTEXT.md).
