# Settings

Your NextFleet settings are in **Personal settings** > **Additional settings**, section **NextFleet**.
**Settings** in the NextFleet navigation opens them.
Each setting saves at once; the grid factor when you leave the field.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Country** | The country of the vehicles you add from now on. It sets their units, currency and rules. Your vehicles keep theirs. |
| **I reclaim VAT** | Shows the costs net of VAT, for example for a business. |
| **Grid factor for charging, g CO₂/kWh** | The CO₂ of your electricity. Keep it empty for the average of the country. |
| **Inbox folder** | The folder of the [receipt inbox](documents.md#use-the-receipt-inbox). |

## Connect a phone app or a script

Other apps use the NextFleet API.
They sign in with a Nextcloud app password, never with your password, and they see what you see.

- The app opens the Nextcloud login in your browser.
  You log in and grant access.
- Or you make an app password: in **Personal settings** > **Security** > **Devices & sessions**, type a name and click **Create new app password**.
  Type the login and password into the app.

To stop an app, delete its app password in **Devices & sessions**.
Developers find the API in [api.md](../api.md).
