# Documents and the receipt inbox

You can attach files from your Nextcloud Files to a vehicle: the registration, the insurance, a manual, a receipt or a photo.
A document belongs to the vehicle, or to one entry or booking.

## Attach a document

1. On the vehicle page, in **Documents**, click **Add document**.
2. Select the file and click **Choose**.
3. In **What it is**, select the kind.
4. In **Belongs to**, select an entry or a booking, or keep **The vehicle itself**.
5. Click **Attach**.

You can attach only your own files, not files shared with you.
A driver attaches only to their own entries and bookings.
The owner and the managers also attach to the vehicle itself.

Everyone who sees the vehicle can download its documents.
NextFleet does not copy the file.
It stays in your Files.

> [!WARNING]
> If you delete a file or move it out of your Files, nobody can open it from the vehicle any more.

To remove a document, click **Remove**.
The file stays in your Files.

## Use the receipt inbox

The inbox is a folder in your Files.
Photos and PDF files in it wait until you attach them.
Point the automatic upload of the Nextcloud mobile app at it, and a photo of a receipt goes straight to NextFleet.

### Select the folder

1. Click **Settings** in the navigation.
2. In **Inbox folder**, click **Choose folder** and select a folder of your own.
3. In the Nextcloud mobile app, set the automatic upload to this folder.

The navigation now shows **Inbox** with the number of waiting files.
Files in subfolders do not show.

### Attach a file from the inbox

1. Click **Inbox**, then a file.
2. Select the **Vehicle**, **What it is** and **Belongs to**.
3. Click **Attach**.

For a cost that you did not record yet, click **New fill-up**, **New maintenance** or **New expense**.
The entry form opens.
When you save, NextFleet attaches the file to the new entry.

NextFleet never moves or deletes the files in the inbox.
