# Reminders

A reminder tells you when a service, an inspection or another task is due: on a date, at a counter reading, or at whichever comes first.

The owner and the managers add, change, snooze and skip reminders.

## Add a reminder

1. On the vehicle page, click **+ Reminder**.
2. In **Reminder**, select a template, for example **Oil change**.
   For your own title, keep the list empty and type the **Title**.
3. In **Due by**, select **Date**, **Counter** or **Date or counter, whichever comes first**.
   Type the due date or reading.
4. To repeat it, type the interval in **Repeat every**.
5. Select the warnings: a month before, at the start of the month, on the day, or a distance before.
6. Click **Add reminder**.

For a German vehicle without an HU/AU reminder, the vehicle page asks **When is the next HU/AU?**
Type the month and year from the sticker on the plate.

## The states

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Planned** | Not near yet. |
| **Coming up** | The warning time started. |
| **Due** | The date or the reading is reached. |
| **Overdue** | The date is past. A counter reminder stays **Due**. |
| **Snoozed** | You moved it to a later date. |
| **Done**, **Skipped** | It does not repeat and leaves the list. |

A reminder that repeats moves on to its next due date.
For a counter reminder, **Expected around** shows a date from your readings, or **Not enough data yet**.

A reminder pauses while the vehicle is laid up and stops when it is disposed of.

## Mark a reminder as done

Drivers can do this too.

1. Click **Done** on the reminder.

   The entry form opens with a maintenance record for it.
2. Fill in the record.
   A reminder that repeats by distance needs the **Counter reading**.
3. Click **Save**.

The next time is counted from the date and reading of the record, not from the planned date.

## Snooze or skip a reminder

Click the reminder, then **Snooze 1 week**, **Snooze 1 month** or **Snooze until** a date.

**Skip this time** skips one occurrence.
A repeated reminder comes back next time.
To end a reminder for good, click **Delete reminder**.

## How you hear about it

- **Notifications** in Nextcloud and on your phone, at each warning you selected.
- **Reminder mail**, at most one a day.
  Each vehicle sets how often: **No mail**, **Daily**, **Weekly, on Monday** (the default) or **Monthly, on the 1st**.
- **Dashboard**: the **Vehicle reminders** widget shows what is coming up, due or overdue.

## Who gets them

In **Edit vehicle**, **Reminders go to** lists who gets the notifications and mails.
The owner is on it at the start.
A change to the list saves at once.

Being on the list gives no access to the vehicle.
You can add users that your administrator lets you share with, and users who already have access.
